## 2. Context & Problem Statement

Prior worldbuilding established the core setting of **Atlas** and the narrative epic **The Undertow**, but:
1. **Genre / Scaffold Borrowing:** Early world generation scripts and economic tables borrowed heavily from Lineage 2 names (*Adena, Mammon, Giran, etc.*). The project needed its own distinct vocabulary and branding.
2. **The Player Protagonist Paradox (Veto 1):** In an MMO / shared-world architecture, thousands of players exist simultaneously. A traditional single-player "Chosen One" trope breaks immersion. The game required an open, plural archetype title that **allows anyone to be one** (similar to *The Tarnished*, *The Guardian*, or *Jedi Knight*), while still feeling heroic and prestigious.
3. **Memorability:** Many internal worldbuilding names (*Cindervast, Wealdmarch, Stonemoor*) are long and hard to remember. The brand required a punchy, evocative, internationally recognizable title.

---

## 3. Etymology & Lore Foundation

* **Atlas:** The overarching universe, world engine, and charted continent system.
* **Cala:** Authentic High Elven (*Quenya*) word for **"Light" / "Radiance"**.
* **Knight:** The universal, grounded English term for a dedicated martial protector, sentinel, and oath-bound champion.
* **The Cala Knight (Plural: *The Cala Knights*):**
  * Literally translates to **"Knight of Light"** / **"Protector of the Light"**.
  * Represents an order, mantle, or sacred path that players take up across the frontier to safeguard the remnants of civilization, hold back the Void, and protect the unsealed world.
