# Decision: Cala Knight Is an Exclusive Legendary Title

**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Status:** Approved (owner "แบบ 2", 2026-11-07); amended 2026-11-07 (F-179: the Last Cala Knight)
**Tier:** R0 / Brand & Narrative Core
**Builds on:** [2026-11-07-game-title-and-player-identity.md](2026-11-07-game-title-and-player-identity.md), [2026-11-07-own-vocabulary-glossary.md](2026-11-07-own-vocabulary-glossary.md)
**Supersedes:** items 2 and 3 of §4 in `2026-11-07-game-title-and-player-identity.md` (player is a Cala Knight; Cala Knights are the player order). Item 1 (the title `Atlas: The Cala Knight`) stays.

---

## 1. Decision

1. **Cala Knight is a rare legendary title** held by a handful of historical heroes. It is numbered **by person** internally (an ordinal counts holders, not eras), but **no ordinal is ever stated in player-facing text**; how many there were stays unknown.
2. **Present-day players never earn it.** Players are ordinary independent adventurers, one of thousands.
3. **Expansion I** retraces the unfinished story of the **Last Cala Knight** (owner, 2026-11-07: "last Cala Knight, no need to specify number"). She is a woman. To players she is mysterious: name, race, era and the unfinished thing surface only through traces. Writers' bible: personality like Himmel (Frieren), strength like Serie, the strongest of her age; a benchmark, never a borrowed name, line or design. The story engine is Frieren/Himmel style (a legend followed through the people they left behind); no names are borrowed.
4. **Expansion II and III following earlier Cala Knights are proposals**, not commitments. Individual Cala Knights' identities and timelines are not locked.
5. **Optional player faction:** the **Kilnwatch Wardens**, based at Kilnwatch Bastion and joinable at Lv 10. Joining is offered, never forced.

## 2. Reasoning

- It fits the owner's rule that **the player is not the main character**. A shared-world MMO cannot make thousands of players the one chosen hero.
- The game title names the person the player chases: `Atlas: The Cala Knight` points at the Last Cala Knight, not at the player.
- The old item 2/3 wording ("the player is a Cala Knight", "the order the player joins") reintroduced the chosen-one trap that the glossary tone rules reject.

## 3. Effects

- Glossary §4: the Cala Knight row becomes the legendary title; the Kilnwatch Wardens row is added.
- Glossary §3 opening and the six race invitation lines no longer say the player is a Cala Knight. The pitch, convergence row and invitations are drafted in F-178 spec §1-§3.
- The 9-chapter structure, NPCs, birth token, Volunteers' Road and the Ch VII rule are unaffected.

## 4. Open items

1. The Last Cala Knight's name, race, era and unfinished deed; identities of the earlier holders.
2. Whether Exp II and Exp III proceed at all.
3. Kilnwatch Wardens' ranks, rewards and whether any gameplay is gated behind joining (default: none).
