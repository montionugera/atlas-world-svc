## One-line summary

The seal attests that **a statement was given to the Bellfaith and recorded** — never that it
is true. Forgery is possible and worthless. No new magic; no new access rule, because
`canon.md:253-254` already provides the gate.

## Why the folder slug is wrong

The idea was captured as *"why the seal cannot be usefully forged — magic allowed but
bounded"*. The brainstorm landed somewhere better: **no new magic is needed at all**, because
forgery was never the attack surface. The catalog titles are corrected; the folder slug still
carries the obsolete framing and is left alone rather than renamed mid-release.
