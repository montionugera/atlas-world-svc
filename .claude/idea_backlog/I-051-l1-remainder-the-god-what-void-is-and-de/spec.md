# L1 cosmology — the unsealed years

## Problem

The world has **no cosmology at all**, and this is not an inference from silence about one deity.
A grep across every content file for `god`, `gods`, `divine`, `deity`, `pantheon`, `creation
myth`, `heaven`, `afterlife`, `soul` returns **zero hits** (`A0-current-world.md:35`).

Three concrete consequences:

1. **Void has a mechanic and no metaphysics.** It grows from unburied battlefields, a bell answers
   it, it is taught from unlicensed manuals — and **nothing states what it is** (`A0:42`). It is
   simultaneously the most-used element in the corpus (28 uses) and the least explained.
2. **The world has no deep time.** The oldest thing in it is a caravan at ~100 years; the deepest
   history is one generation back (`A0:380`, G2). The relic weapon is called *ancient* by a world
   that has no ancient (`A0:391`, G13).
3. **The Bellfaith is an institution with no doctrine** — bells, wardens, a seal, a school, a tower
   district, and no stated belief and no object of worship (`A0:38`).

## Why now

Wave 4 is complete (F-029 ecology, F-030 boss, F-031 promote-monsters). Wave 5 is this idea alone,
and `idea-map.md:91` records that L1 cosmology **informs L3** — bosses, dungeons and camps. Every
wave after this one authors content on ground this idea is supposed to have defined. Writing it
after L3 means retrofitting meaning onto monsters that already shipped.

The research half is already paid for: both dossiers under `docs/research/` exist and cover all
six SWF §5 research lines, so this idea starts at brainstorm, not at zero.

## Sketch

Join two facts that have sat on separate pages of `canon.md` since the beginning — **the relic
weapon erases rather than burns, leaving no body to bury**, and **Void grows from the unburied
dead**. The join yields a deep past, an origin for Void, and an explanation for the world's own
short memory, without inventing a new force and without a god.

Deliverables, owner-approved: `docs/worldbuilding/A1-cosmology.md`; five lore nodes on a new
`the-unsealed-years` thread; a canon chronology entry; removal of the king theme across the
working docs **and the shipped novel**; `DR-006` settling SWF §7.

Full detail, including the fifteen removal sites and the gate self-check, is in the design.
