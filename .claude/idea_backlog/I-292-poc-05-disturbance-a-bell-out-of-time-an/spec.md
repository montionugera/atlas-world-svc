# I-292 POC-05: Disturbance — A Bell Out of Time & The Impossible Flame (07:45–10:00)

## Goal
End the 10-minute experience with a clear supernatural time anomaly (`q-maro-05` + `q-maro-06` **Time-Wound** climax) while keeping the atmosphere hopeful and leaving the player curious to explore beyond Maro Bell.

## Player-Facing Experience (07:45–10:00)
**Edda** (Scar, 46, bell schedule keeper) notices an impossible discrepancy in The Great Bell's timing (`q-maro-05` beat). Moments later, a nearby lantern suffers a **Time-Wound** (`q-maro-06` *The Hungry Flame* beat) — burning through its oil in seconds with synchronized light and audio effects (and an optional environmental glimpse of an **Oil Leech**). The player helps stabilize the immediate moment and receives an ordinary, warm invitation to continue helping beyond the Bell.

## Scope In vs Out
- **Scope In:** **Edda** NPC interaction & timing-anomaly dialogue, Great Bell audio/clock discrepancy cue, deterministic replayable **Time-Wound** lantern oil-drain event, synchronized light/audio state transitions, brief player stabilization action, optional visual glimpse of **Oil Leech**, and closing travel hook.
- **Scope Out:** Explaining the Great Kilns or Chapter III/V secrets, full Oil Leech combat encounter, large boss fight, or mandatory Kilnwatch Wardens faction recruitment.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Edda** dialogue and Great Bell timing cue transition cleanly into the deterministic **Time-Wound** lantern oil-drain event.
- [ ] The Time-Wound lantern light/oil transition and audio cues fire reliably across repeated runs and cannot be duplicated via reconnects.
- [ ] Closing beat invites further exploration beyond Maro Bell without lore-dumping the cause of the time anomaly or forcing faction membership.
