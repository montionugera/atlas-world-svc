## (c) Kilnwatch Wardens: what joining gives

- **Joining quest** `q-warden-join-01` "A Place on the Watch" (Lv 10): repair a damaged signal-post lantern, deliver the incident report, sign the Wardens' public service ledger. No exam, no prophecy, no birth check.
- **Ranks** **[C: renamed; "Steward" reads as airline crew in Thai, "Keeper" collides with Rena the Memory Keeper]**:

| Rank | Requirement | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runner (รันเนอร์) | joining quest | Warden board quests, +10 storage slots, faction affiliation title |
| Warden (วอร์เดน) | 100 Warden reputation | +20 storage total, Warden cloak cosmetic, 5% discount on Warden consumables |
| Elder Warden (เอลเดอร์ วอร์เดน) | 350 Warden reputation | +30 storage total, lantern accessory cosmetic, convenience teleport to Kilnwatch Bastion |

- Reputation comes only from repeatable community-service board quests, never from main-story progress.
- **Never gated:** main story and chapters, dungeons, raids, world bosses, markets, ordinary storage, equipment progression, every Cala Knight discovery. No faction-exclusive combat power.
- **Leave / return:** resign at the registrar any time; rank and reputation are kept, bonuses pause and resume on rejoining; no fee.
- **Legend link:** the Wardens keep an incomplete ledger associated with the Last Cala Knight; membership gives no claim to her title.
