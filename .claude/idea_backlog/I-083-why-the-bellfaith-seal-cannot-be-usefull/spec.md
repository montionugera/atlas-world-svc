## One-line summary

The seal certifies **provenance, not truth** — it attests that two witnesses from two
different towns said a thing, never that the thing is accurate. Forgery is possible and
worthless; the exploitable flaw is the testimony rule, and the Broker is the only party in
the land who can reach two towns at once.

## Why the title is now half-wrong

The idea was captured as *"why the seal cannot be usefully forged — magic allowed but
bounded"*. The brainstorm landed somewhere better: **no new magic is needed at all**, because
forgery was never the attack. The owner proposed the fake-witness framing, which dissolved the
magic question instead of answering it.

The design does still carry a magic-adjacent fix — `A0-current-world.md:337` (V16) attributes
"wax seals crack only when tampered with" to `canon.md:280-285`, where it does not appear.
That citation defect ships with the change.
