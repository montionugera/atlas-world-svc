# Iron Regent's replacement motive

## Problem

I-051 removed the land-wide taboo that made "king" a curse word. The Iron Regent's deepest secret
was built directly on that taboo — he wanted to be **"the second king of a land that curses the
word king."** With the taboo gone, that sentence no longer means anything, so it was replaced with
**"the only power left in a land that will not have a centre."**

The replacement is coherent and it is not a hole — but it is **blunter**. The original had a
specific, ironic edge: the man's ambition was defined by the exact word his society had made
unspeakable. The replacement is a more ordinary statement of wanting power.

Concrete sites now carrying the blunter line:

- `docs/story/undertow/core-story.md:138` — his deepest secret, in the character list
- `docs/story/undertow/core-story.md:190` — the act-4 relic deal
- `docs/story/undertow/novel-complete.md:582`, `:636` — the shipped novel
- `docs/story/undertow/novel-illustrated-edition.html:693` — his portrait card
- `docs/story/undertow/glossary-th.md:70`

This matters because he is the buyer in act 4's relic sale, which is the moral weight of the
whole act. A blunt motive there costs more than a blunt motive anywhere else in the corpus.

## Why now

Not urgent — the current text is consistent and ships fine. The owner explicitly agreed during the
I-051 brainstorm that holes could be filled later, and this was the hole named at the time.

The reason to do it before wave 6+ authors more content: every new quest or dialogue line that
references his ambition will be written against whatever phrasing is current. The longer the blunt
version stands, the more content derives from it.

## Sketch

Find an ambition as specific as the removed one, using something the world still has. Candidates
worth exploring, none decided:

- Something built on the **Bellfaith's seal** — the man who wants to be the thing that certifies
  what is true, rather than the thing that rules. The seal is now the oldest and most valuable
  instrument in the world (`A1-cosmology.md` C5), and the Bell-Keeper's crime already proves it can
  be captured.
- Something built on the **relic supply being fixed** (`A1-cosmology.md` C6) — he does not want to
  rule, he wants to be the only person who holds one.
- Something built on his sister. His current motive routes entirely around the War-Countess without
  ever being *about* her.

Do not reach back for a king framing in a new costume. The whole point of the removal was that the
world refuses a centre, not a word.
