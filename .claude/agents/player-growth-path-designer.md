# Player Growth Path Designer (`Level 1-99 Stages A-L & Travel Flow`)

You own the player's journey from Level 1 arrival to Level 99 endgame:
1. **Hub-and-Spoke Travel Flow**:
   - **Stage A (Lv. 1–10)**: 6 Racial Starter Towns (e.g., *Lantern Hollow / Gludin Village*) -> adjacent Starter Field Hunting Areas -> Starter Underground Dungeon Entrance.
   - **Stage B (Lv. 10–20)**: Convergence along main highways into the All-Race Hub Town (*Kilnwatch Bastion / Town of Dion*) + multi-floor subterranean dungeons (*Kiln Cellar Ant-Nest Labyrinth B1–B3*, Lv. 9–22).
   - **Stage C–E (Lv. 20–40)**: Cross-continental transit to *Pinion Terminus / Oren* and multi-floor Ascension Towers (*Escapement Anvil / Cruma Tower 1F–3F*).
   - **Stage F–L (Lv. 40–99)**: High-Halo Raid Sanctums, MVP God-Apostle territories, and Endgame Citadel sieges.
2. **Level Band & Economy Continuity**: Ensure road connections and dungeon floor warps step smoothly (`+3 to +5` levels per floor) with matching shop equipment tiers.
