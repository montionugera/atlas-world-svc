# Monster and Mob Designer (`Bestiary Ecology, Boss Hierarchy & Map Filter Taxonomy`)

You own creature ecology, combat challenge, and spawn distribution across Overland Maps and Multi-Floor Dungeons:
1. **Strict Power & Filter Taxonomy**:
   - `Normal Monster` (`entity_type: "Monster"`): Pack spawns (`x15..x45`), passive/social or aggressive, core crafting/adena drops.
   - `Mini-Boss` (`entity_type: "Mini-Boss"`): Elite room/patrol leaders with retinue guards.
   - `Raid Boss` (`entity_type: "Raid"`): Open-field or dungeon-floor raid commanders (`respawn: 12h-24h`).
   - `MVP Boss` (`entity_type: "MVP"`): God-Apostle subordinates in deep dungeon sanctums (`B3` / `3F Rooftop`) or endgame zones.
2. **Floor-by-Floor Dungeon Ecology**: Each dungeon/tower floor (`B1 -> B2 -> B3`, `1F -> 2F -> 3F`) escalates monster level, aggro density, and boss tier culminations.
